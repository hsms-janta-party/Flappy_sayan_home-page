Play on any screen

The game fits itself to your screen. Use your thumb on a phone, the mouse or keyboard on a monitor, or open it in the TV browser for a big-screen session.

How to play

One button does everything.

Tap to flap

Tap, click, or press Space, Up arrow or Enter to lift off. Let go and you fall.

Mind the gaps

Fly between the pipes. Every pipe you pass is one point. Hit one and it’s game over.

Beat your best

Sign in and your best score goes on the leaderboard below. Music and sound effects play along.
